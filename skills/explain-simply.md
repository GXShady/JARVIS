---
name: explain-simply
description: Explain a concept clearly. Use for "explain X", "how does X work", "what is X", "explain it like I'm five".
---

1. Pitch it to what you know of their work from memory: skip what they certainly know, define what they may not.
2. One sentence of what it is. One sentence with a concrete everyday analogy. Stop.
3. If the idea has a shape — a cycle, a pipeline, a hierarchy, a comparison — draw it on a blade with `display` rather than describing it.
4. If they say "go deeper", add one layer at a time, each still two sentences.
