---
name: brainstorm
description: Generate ideas. Use for "brainstorm", "give me ideas for", "help me think of", "name ideas for".
---

1. Ask for the constraint that matters most if they gave none. One question.
2. Produce twelve ideas across at least four different angles — do not give twelve variations of one.
3. Blade: the ideas grouped by angle, your three strongest marked.
4. Speak your top pick and what makes it the one.
5. On "more like that", go deeper on the angle they chose rather than starting again.
