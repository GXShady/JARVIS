---
name: lesson-plan
description: Plan how to learn a skill or subject. Use for "teach me X", "how do I learn X", "make me a study plan for".
---

1. Need: where they are starting from and how many hours a week they have. One question.
2. Research how the subject is best learned and the two or three resources practitioners actually recommend.
3. Blade: a week-by-week plan — what to learn, what to build or practise, how to tell it has been learned.
4. Speak what week one is and what they will be able to do at the end.
5. `remember` that they are learning it and when they started.
