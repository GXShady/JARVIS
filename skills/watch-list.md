---
name: watch-list
description: What to watch, read or listen to. Use for "what should I watch", "recommend a film", "find me a book", "something to listen to".
---

1. Take tastes and what they have already seen from memory.
2. Ask what mood they are in if they did not say. One question.
3. Recommend ONE, with two runners-up. Check where it can currently be watched or bought.
4. Blade: the poster or cover, a two-line reason it suits them, where to find it.
5. Speak the title and the reason.
6. If they report back, `remember` whether they liked it.
