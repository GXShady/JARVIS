---
name: meeting-prep
description: Prepare for a meeting or call. Use for "prep me for my meeting", "who am I meeting", "get me ready for the call with".
---

1. Find the meeting: the one they named, else the next on the calendar if a calendar tool is connected.
2. For each person and company: what memory holds, then a search for role, recent news and anything they have published lately.
3. If mail is connected, read the recent thread with them for open questions and promises made.
4. Blade: Who, Context, What they are likely to want, Three questions to ask, What to settle before leaving the room.
5. Speak the one thing to walk in knowing.
