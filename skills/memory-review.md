---
name: memory-review
description: Read back and tidy what is remembered. Use for "what do you know about me", "what do you remember", "review your memory", "clean up your memory".
---

1. Call `recall` with an empty query, then again with any subject they named.
2. Put everything on one blade with `display`, grouped under plain headings (About you, People, Work, Preferences, Other). One line per memory.
3. Say how many memories there are and name the two or three most significant. Do not read the list.
4. If they asked to tidy: point out entries that contradict each other or are plainly out of date, one at a time, and ask whether to forget each. Use `forget` or `remember` with `replaces` on their answer. Never remove anything they did not confirm.
