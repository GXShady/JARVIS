---
name: deep-research
description: Thorough, multi-source research into a question. Use for "research X", "look into X properly", "find out everything about X", "write me a report on X".
---

1. Restate the question to yourself as three to five sub-questions. Do not say them.
2. Search each from several angles. Read the primary sources themselves — the paper, the filing, the documentation — not summaries of them. Aim for at least six independent sources.
3. Note where sources disagree and which is better placed to know. Throw out claims only one weak source makes.
4. Compose a report blade with `display`: a two-line answer at the top, then Findings, Where sources disagree, What is still unknown. Short source tags beside the claims they support.
5. Speak the answer in two sentences, including how confident the evidence allows you to be.
