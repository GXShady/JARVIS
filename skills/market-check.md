---
name: market-check
description: Prices and market moves. Use for "how is X stock doing", "bitcoin price", "how are the markets", "check my watchlist".
---

1. Which instruments: the ones named; otherwise their watchlist from memory; otherwise the main indices.
2. Use a market-data tool if one is connected, else search for the latest quote. Note the time of the quote.
3. Blade: one row each — price, change on the day, change on the year.
4. Speak the move as words — "up two point one percent" — and the reason only if there is a clear one in today's news.
5. If they say "add X to my watchlist", `remember` it. Never give a buy or sell instruction; give the facts.
