---
name: fact-check
description: Verify a claim. Use for "is it true that", "fact-check this", "did X really say", "is this real".
---

1. State the claim to yourself precisely: who, what, when, how much.
2. Look for the original — the speech, study, dataset or document — rather than coverage of it. Then look for a reputable source that disputes it.
3. Verdict, one of: true, mostly true, misleading, false, unverified.
4. Speak the verdict as a full sentence and the single strongest reason. If it is misleading, say what the accurate version is.
5. Put the evidence on a blade only if they ask to see it.
