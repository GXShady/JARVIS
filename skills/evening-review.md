---
name: evening-review
description: End-of-day wrap-up. Use for "wrap up my day", "end of day", "daily review", "good night".
---

1. Ask what got done today and what did not. One question, then listen.
2. If a calendar tool is connected, read tomorrow's events.
3. Put a blade up with three sections: Done, Carried over, Tomorrow.
4. `remember` anything lasting they mentioned — a decision made, a deadline, a commitment to someone — each as its own dated fact. Do not save the day's trivia.
5. Speak one sentence naming the first thing tomorrow.
