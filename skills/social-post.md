---
name: social-post
description: Write a post for social media. Use for "write a post about", "draft a tweet", "LinkedIn post", "caption for this".
---

1. Need: the platform and the point. Take their work, audience and tone from memory.
2. Write three versions with genuinely different openings: one plain, one with a story, one with a sharp claim. Fit the platform's length and conventions. No hashtag clutter.
3. Blade: the three side by side.
4. Speak which you would post and why, in one sentence.
5. Never post it. Posting needs their explicit word and write access.
