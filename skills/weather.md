---
name: weather
description: Forecast. Use for "what's the weather", "do I need a coat", "will it rain", "weather this weekend".
---

1. Place: the one they named, otherwise their city from memory. If unknown, ask, then `remember` it.
2. Search for the current forecast from a meteorological source.
3. Answer the question they actually asked — "No rain until evening, sir" — in one sentence, with temperatures spoken as words.
4. Only put a blade up if they asked for several days: one row per day with high, low and conditions.
