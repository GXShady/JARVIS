---
name: summarise-page
description: Summarise an article, page, video page or document. Use for "summarise this", "what does this page say", "give me the gist", "tl;dr".
---

1. Which page: the link they gave; otherwise the tab open in their browser (`chrome_*`); otherwise the blade in front.
2. Read the whole thing, not the first screen.
3. Blade: the title, a three-sentence summary, then five to seven key points, then anything the piece claims without support.
4. Speak the core argument in one sentence and whether it is worth their time to read in full.
