---
name: news-briefing
description: Current news on a topic or in general. Use for "what's the news", "what's happening in X", "catch me up on X", "headlines".
---

1. Topic: the one they named, otherwise their field of work from memory, otherwise world news.
2. Search for stories from the last two or three days. Prefer primary reporting over aggregators. Discard anything you cannot date.
3. Put five to seven stories on one blade with `display`: a headline, one sentence of what happened, a short source tag such as REUTERS, and a thumbnail where the story has one.
4. Speak the single most consequential development in one sentence, and a second only if two stories are connected.
5. If they pick a story, open the article itself as a blade in reading mode.
