---
name: translate
description: Translate and help with a language. Use for "how do you say", "translate this", "what does X mean in", "say this in".
---

1. Give the translation as a full sentence: "In Spanish it is ..., sir."
2. Put the phrase on a small blade with a plain pronunciation guide beneath it, and a more formal or casual variant if the choice matters.
3. For a longer text, translate the whole of it on a blade and speak only what it is about.
4. If the phrase could be taken two ways, give the likelier and mention the other in five words.
