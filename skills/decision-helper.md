---
name: decision-helper
description: Think a decision through. Use for "help me decide", "should I", "I can't decide between", "talk me through this choice".
---

1. Ask ONE question: what matters most in this choice. Wait for the answer.
2. Take account of what memory holds about their goals and circumstances.
3. Blade: for each option, what it gains, what it costs, what is irreversible about it.
4. Give a recommendation — you do have one — in one sentence, and the assumption it rests on.
5. If they decide, `remember` the decision with today's date.
