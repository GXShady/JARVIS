---
name: workout-plan
description: Build or run a training session. Use for "give me a workout", "plan my training", "what should I train today".
---

1. Take their goal, equipment, injuries and usual schedule from memory; ask one question for what is missing and `remember` the answers.
2. Build a session that fits the time they have: warm-up, main work with sets, repetitions and rest, cool-down.
3. Blade: the session as a table.
4. Speak the focus of the session and how long it takes.
5. If they want it run, call one exercise per turn and wait for "done".
6. If the camera is on and they ask for a form check, use `watch` on one repetition and give one correction.
