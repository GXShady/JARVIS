---
name: compare-options
description: Compare products, tools, plans or choices side by side. Use for "compare X and Y", "which is better", "X versus Y", "what are my options for".
---

1. Settle the criteria: the ones they named, plus price and the two that usually decide this kind of choice. Use any preference in memory.
2. Research each option against every criterion. Current prices and specifications, dated.
3. Blade: a table, options across, criteria down, the stronger cell in each row marked.
4. Speak a recommendation and the one reason, then the condition under which the other choice wins.
