---
name: morning-briefing
description: The start-of-day briefing. Use for "good morning", "brief me", "what have I got today", "start my day".
---

1. Take their city, work and current projects from memory. If the city is unknown, ask once and `remember` it.
2. Gather, in parallel where you can: today's weather for their city; today's calendar, if a calendar tool is connected; unread mail that needs them, if a mail tool is connected; three headlines in the fields they care about.
3. Compose ONE blade with `display`: the date as the heading, then Weather, Today, Mail, News — each a few short lines. Leave out any section you could not fill; do not write "unavailable".
4. Speak two sentences: the single thing that most deserves their attention today, and the weather only if it will change their plans.
