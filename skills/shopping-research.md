---
name: shopping-research
description: Find the right thing to buy. Use for "find me a good X", "what's the best X under", "should I buy X", "where is X cheapest".
---

1. Need: what it is for and the budget. Ask one question if the budget is unknown.
2. Find three to five candidates from recent independent reviews, not retailer listings. Check current prices at two or more sellers, and whether a newer model is imminent.
3. Blade: a card per candidate with a picture, the price, who it suits and its main weakness.
4. Speak the pick and why, and the cheaper alternative if the difference is small.
5. Never add to a basket or buy.
