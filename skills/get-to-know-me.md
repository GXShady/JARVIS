---
name: get-to-know-me
description: Onboarding interview that learns who the user is. Use for "get to know me", "set yourself up", "learn about me", or when memory is empty and they ask what you know about them.
---

1. Say in one sentence that you will ask a few questions and remember the answers.
2. Ask ONE question per turn, and wait. Cover, in this order, skipping anything already in memory: what to call them; where they live (city, for weather and time); what they do for work; what they are working towards this year; the people who matter (names and relationship); how they like answers (brief or detailed, blunt or gentle); daily routine (when they start, when they stop); interests.
3. After each answer, call `remember` once per fact — separate facts are separate calls — then ask the next question. Do not comment on the answer.
4. If they say "skip" or "enough", stop asking at once.
5. Finish with one sentence saying how many things you now know. Do not read them back.
