---
name: day-planner
description: Plan the day around what must get done. Use for "plan my day", "help me prioritise", "what should I work on", "I have too much to do".
---

1. Ask for everything on their plate. Let them finish the list.
2. If a calendar is connected, read today's fixed commitments.
3. Pick the three that matter most against the goals in memory. Be willing to say what should be dropped.
4. Blade: a timed plan for the day fitted round the fixed events, hardest work at the hour they work best (from memory, else the morning), with breaks.
5. Speak the first task and when to start it.
