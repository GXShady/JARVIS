---
name: gift-ideas
description: Suggest a gift for someone. Use for "what should I get X", "gift ideas for", "it's X's birthday".
---

1. `recall` the person by name for their interests, the relationship and past gifts. Ask for the budget and the occasion if unknown.
2. `remember` anything new they tell you about the person.
3. Find five gifts that fit this particular person — with current prices and where to buy — and avoid anything generic.
4. Blade: a card each with a picture, price and one line on why it suits them.
5. Speak the one you would choose. When they decide, `remember` what was given and when.
