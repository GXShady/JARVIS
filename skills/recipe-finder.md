---
name: recipe-finder
description: Decide what to cook. Use for "what can I make with", "find me a recipe for", "what's for dinner", "how do I cook".
---

1. Take diet, allergies and dislikes from memory; if they mention a new one, `remember` it.
2. Find one recipe that fits what they have and the time they have. One, not a list.
3. Blade: the dish, a photograph, ingredients with quantities, numbered steps with timings.
4. Speak the name and the total time.
5. If they say "walk me through it", read ONE step per turn and wait for "next".
