---
name: form-check
description: Watch the user do something physical and coach it. Use for "check my form", "am I doing this right", "watch me", "how does this look".
---

1. Open the camera as a blade if it is not open, and say "Go ahead, sir."
2. `watch` for six to ten seconds, covering one full repetition or attempt.
3. Read the frames as a sequence. Find the ONE fault that matters most.
4. Speak one correction as an instruction about what to do, not what was wrong: "Elbows closer to the body, sir."
5. Offer to watch again. Never list several corrections at once.
