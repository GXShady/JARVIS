---
name: money-check
description: Quick calculations about money. Use for "can I afford", "how much is X a month", "split the bill", "convert X to", "what's the tip".
---

1. Do the arithmetic exactly. For currency, look up today's rate and say it is today's.
2. Speak the answer as a full sentence with the figure in words.
3. For anything with more than three numbers — a loan schedule, a budget, a comparison over time — put the working on a blade as a table.
4. State any assumption you had to make in five words.
