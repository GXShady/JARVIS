---
name: plan-project
description: Turn a goal into a plan. Use for "plan this project", "break this down", "how do I get X done", "make me a plan for".
---

1. If the deadline or the definition of done is missing, ask for it — one question only.
2. Break the goal into three to six phases, each with concrete tasks someone could start on tomorrow, an owner if others are involved, and a date.
3. Mark the critical path and the one risk most likely to slip the date.
4. Blade: phases as sections, tasks as rows with dates.
5. Speak the first action and the date the whole thing lands.
6. `remember` the project, its goal and its deadline as one fact.
