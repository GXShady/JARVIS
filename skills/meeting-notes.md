---
name: meeting-notes
description: Turn a spoken recap into notes and actions. Use for "take notes", "log this meeting", "here's what we discussed".
---

1. Let them talk. Do not interrupt; acknowledge with two words at most.
2. When they finish, produce a blade: Decisions, Actions (who, what, by when), Open questions.
3. Ask about any action with no owner or no date — one question covering all of them.
4. `remember` each commitment THEY made, with its date.
5. If write access is on and they ask, save the notes as a file in the workspace.
