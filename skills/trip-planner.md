---
name: trip-planner
description: Plan travel. Use for "plan a trip to", "weekend in X", "what should I do in X", "build me an itinerary".
---

1. Need: destination, dates, who is going. Take home city, budget habits and interests from memory. Ask one question for what is missing.
2. Research: how to get there and roughly what it costs, the area to stay in, and things to do that fit their interests, with current opening times.
3. Blade: a day-by-day itinerary, morning, afternoon and evening, with travel times between stops and a rough total cost.
4. Speak the shape of the trip in one sentence and the one thing that must be booked ahead.
5. Never book or pay for anything.
