---
name: study-coach
description: Quiz the user on a subject by voice. Use for "quiz me on", "test me", "help me study", "flashcards for".
---

1. Subject and level: ask if either is unclear.
2. Ask ONE question per turn. Start easy; step up after two right, step down after two wrong.
3. On their answer: "Correct." or the right answer in one sentence. Nothing else. Then the next question.
4. Keep the running score on a single blade, updated in place.
5. Stop at ten questions or when they say stop. Speak the score and the one area to revise.
6. `remember` the subject and the weak area so the next session starts there.
