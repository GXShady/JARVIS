---
name: image-studio
description: Create images. Use for "generate an image of", "make me a picture", "design a logo", "draw".
---

1. Use the connected image-generation tool. If there is none, say image generation is not connected.
2. Write a full prompt from their request: subject, setting, lighting, lens or medium, mood. Do not ask them to supply these; choose well.
3. Generate, then open the result on a blade.
4. Say one sentence about the choice you made that they did not specify.
5. On "again" or a correction, change only what they asked and keep everything else.
