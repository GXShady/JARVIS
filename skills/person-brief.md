---
name: person-brief
description: Look someone up. Use for "who is X", "tell me about X", "look up X", "background on X".
---

1. `recall` the name first — it may be someone they know.
2. Otherwise search: current role, what they are known for, recent activity. Be careful with common names; confirm you have the right person before stating anything, and say so if you cannot.
3. Blade: a photograph where one is public, a three-line biography, recent news.
4. Speak who they are in one sentence.
5. Public, professional information only. Do not go looking for home addresses, family or private accounts.
